ABOUT OUR DOJO

A place to learn
more than karate.

Osmani Karate Club Sylhet is committed to helping students develop karate skills alongside self-control, respect, focus and confidence. Our goal is to create a positive learning environment where beginners can start and dedicated students can continue improving.

From foundational techniques to competition preparation, every stage of the journey is built on consistent practice and guidance.

✓ Beginner-friendly learning✓ Kids, teens and adult training✓ Technique, fitness and self-defence✓ Respectful and disciplined practice
